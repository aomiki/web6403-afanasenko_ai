AS17-148-22727 · Apollo 17 · 1972

The Blue Marble

The Apollo 17 crew photographed Earth on December 7, 1972, on their way to the Moon. Africa, the Arabian Peninsula and the Antarctic ice cap are visible.

ISS056-E-201248 · Soyuz MS-08 · 2018

The ISS above Earth

Expedition 56 crew members photographed the International Space Station from their Soyuz after undocking on October 4, 2018, before returning home after 197 days in space.

Original at NASA

ISS007-E-07306 · ISS Expedition 7 · 2003

The Edge of the Atmosphere

That thin blue line is our planet's entire atmosphere. Astronaut Edward Lu took this photo through an ISS window on June 13, 2003.

Original at NASA
